About Al Fima Agency

Building safe, legal pathways for Kenyan talent since day one

Our Mission

Every placement is safe, legal, and verified

Al Fima Agency Ltd is a fully licensed recruitment and human resource company based in Nairobi. We exist to connect qualified Kenyan job seekers with genuine, vetted employers abroad — never through shortcuts, and never at the expense of a candidate's safety or rights.

From the first interview to the day a candidate lands at their destination, our team manages documentation, employer verification, and pre-departure preparation so that every journey is built on a legitimate contract, not a promise.

Our Services

Three tracks. One rigorous standard.

Track 01

Caregiving & Healthcare

We place trained caregivers and healthcare support staff into hospitals, care homes, and private households across Germany and Canada. Roles include elderly care assistants, hospital support staff, and certified nursing aides.

  • Hospital and elderly care support roles
  • Certification support for destination-country standards
  • Ongoing welfare check-ins after placement
Track 02

General Employment

Our largest category covers logistics, hospitality, and retail roles across Dubai, Saudi Arabia, and beyond — placements that reward reliability and a strong work ethic with stable income and accommodation.

  • Warehouse, logistics, and supply chain roles
  • Hotel, restaurant, and hospitality positions
  • Retail and general labour contracts
Track 03

Household Management

We carefully vet host families and employers before placing housemaids and domestic staff, ensuring fair contracts, respectful working conditions, and clear terms from day one.

  • Verified employer background checks
  • Clear, written contract terms in advance
  • Direct support line during the placement
